Website privacy.

Last updated: 16 August 2026

What we collect

When you request a demo, we collect your name, work email, company and the work you tell us is slowing your team down. We also create salted hashes of your IP address and email address to control abuse. We do not store your raw IP address.

Why we use it

We use these details only to respond to your demo request, protect the form from abuse and keep a record of the request while it is active.

Services involved

Cloudflare protects the website and provides Turnstile bot checks. Supabase stores accepted demo requests in a private, row-level-security-protected database. Our hosting provider processes the form before it is sent to Supabase.

How long we keep it

Spam records are deleted after 30 days. Closed demo requests are deleted after 180 days unless the relationship has moved into an approved customer system. Abuse-control events are deleted after 30 days.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete your demo-request information by emailing privacy@assistall.ai. We may retain limited information when required for security or legal reasons.

Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent to privacy@assistall.ai.
